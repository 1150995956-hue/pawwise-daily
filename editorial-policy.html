HOW WE WORK

Our editorial policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Pawwise Daily publishes practical, general educational information for people who care for dogs and cats. Our aim is to make everyday topics easier to understand while putting animal welfare and safety first.

Research and sources

When a guide discusses animal health, safety or behaviour, we aim to consult reliable veterinary, university, animal-welfare or professional resources and link to useful references in the article. Readers should check the linked source for detailed, individual guidance.

Accuracy and limitations

Pet-care recommendations can vary with an animal’s age, health, breed, environment and individual needs. Our articles are educational starting points, not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for a relationship with a licensed veterinarian. Our current articles have not been individually reviewed by a veterinarian unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Updates and corrections

We may revise articles when guidance, references or site information changes. A displayed update date indicates an editorial update; it does not mean that every statement has been independently re-certified on that date. We aim to correct material errors when identified.

Independence

We aim to explain practical options without promising cures, guaranteed results or miracle products. If paid placements, sponsorships or affiliate links are introduced, they should be clearly identified in accordance with our affiliate and advertising disclosure.

Contact and feedback

This project does not currently publish a dedicated contact email or contact form. We therefore cannot promise a response to individual correction requests. Please use a licensed veterinary professional for time-sensitive or personal animal-health questions.

Reader reminder: If a pet has difficulty breathing, collapses, has a seizure, may have been poisoned, or cannot urinate, seek urgent veterinary care.
