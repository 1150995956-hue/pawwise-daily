GET IN TOUCH

Contact Pawwise Daily

Thank you for reading Pawwise Daily. We welcome thoughtful feedback that helps us make our dog and cat care guides clearer, more accurate and more useful for pet parents.

What you can contact us about

  • Corrections: Tell us if a statement, link, image credit or practical instruction appears inaccurate or out of date. Include the page URL and the specific passage so our editorial team can review it.
  • Topic suggestions: Suggest a dog or cat care question you would like us to research, such as grooming, behaviour, enrichment, nutrition basics or everyday safety.
  • Website issues: Report broken links, display problems, accessibility barriers or pages that are difficult to use on a phone.
  • Editorial and partnership enquiries: Use the public project issue tracker to start a conversation about editorial standards or a potential collaboration. Commercial relationships must be clearly disclosed to readers.

How to send feedback

For public feedback or to report a correction, use the Pawwise Daily feedback and corrections page. Include the relevant article URL and a concise description of the issue. Please do not post private contact details, account information, medical records or other sensitive personal information: public reports may be visible to anyone and may require a GitHub account.

Before asking a pet-health question

Pawwise Daily publishes general educational information. We are not a veterinary clinic and cannot diagnose an animal, recommend treatment for an individual pet or respond to emergencies. If your pet is unwell, injured, in pain or showing sudden behavioural changes, contact a licensed veterinarian. For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, seek emergency veterinary care immediately rather than waiting for a website reply.

We appreciate readers who help us keep our information practical, respectful and responsible. Thank you for helping Pawwise Daily improve.

Important: Our articles are educational and do not replace advice from a veterinarian who can examine your pet.
