OUR PURPOSE

Good information. Kinder pet care.

Pawwise Daily is an independent educational project for people who share their lives with dogs and cats.

Our goal is to make everyday pet care easier to understand: feeding routines, grooming, enrichment, safe home environments, and the value of preventive veterinary care.

We believe useful advice should be clear, considerate, and realistic. Pets have different needs depending on age, breed, health, and personality, so our guides are starting points—not one-size-fits-all instructions.

Our editorial approach

  • Prioritize animal welfare and practical everyday guidance.
  • Use plain language and explain when professional veterinary advice is needed.
  • Do not promise cures, guaranteed outcomes, or miracle products.
  • Review and improve content as the site grows.
  • Clearly distinguish general education from veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not invent expert credentials, testimonials, product tests, or first-hand experiences.

Important: Our articles are general education and are not a substitute for advice from a licensed veterinarian.

How to use our guides

Start with the guide that matches your question, follow the internal links for related topics, and use the linked veterinary and animal-welfare references to explore further. Health and safety decisions should always be checked with a licensed veterinary professional who knows your pet.
