About this policy

This page explains, in general terms, how this informational website handles visitor information. The site is currently a simple static website and does not provide user accounts, comments, or a newsletter sign-up form.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may maintain their own logs and privacy policies.

Pawwise Daily does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies at the time of this update. If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other third-party services are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies as described below.

This website does not currently display Google AdSense ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites.

Visitors can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Before advertising is activated, we intend to configure the required consent choices and advertising settings for visitors in regions where applicable law or Google policy requires them. The services and choices described here will be updated if our practices change.

External links

Our pages may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the relevant third party’s policy before sharing information with it.
